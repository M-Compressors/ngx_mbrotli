Prebuilt deb and rpm packages for amd64 and arm64 on Cloudsmith: `nginx-module-mbrotli` for nginx.org's stable and mainline nginx, on every distribution release nginx.org packages for, and `libnginx-mod-http-mbrotli` and `nginx-mod-mbrotli` for the nginx of Debian, Ubuntu, RHEL-compatible distributions and Fedora, from 1.25.4 on. See "Installation from packages" in the README.
